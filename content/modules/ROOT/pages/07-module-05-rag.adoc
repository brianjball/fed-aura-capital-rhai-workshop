Private data for best results.

Lenora's base model understands mortgage concepts, but Fed Aura Capital's internal policies were never part of its training data.
Retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) gives Lenora relevant passages from trusted documents at request time, allowing her to answer with current, organization-specific facts.

You will first experience RAG in the existing Fed Aura application as the CEO.
Then you will return to GenAI Studio and reproduce the same before-and-after behavior by uploading a policy document yourself.

By the end of this module, you will be able to:

== Exercise 1: Experience RAG in Fed Aura

Open the Fed Aura Capital application and sign in as the *CEO, David Park*.
Open Lenora and send this prompt:

[source,text]
----
What is Fed Aura Capital's maximum back-end DTI ratio for a conventional loan?
Who can approve an exception above that limit, and how often are exception
approvals reviewed by the Risk Committee?
----

Observe that Lenora's response is grounded in *Company Underwriting Manual, Rev. 2024-Q4*.
The response should identify these document-backed facts:

* The standard maximum back-end DTI ratio is *40%*.
* A DTI override up to *45%* can be approved by the Underwriting Manager.
* Major exceptions require approval from the Chief Credit Officer.
* Exception approvals are audited quarterly and reported to the Risk Committee.

Do not treat a fluent answer alone as proof of retrieval.
Look for a source citation or retrieved passage and compare the claims with the source document.

== Exercise 2: Ask Without RAG in GenAI Studio

Now experience the workflow yourself.
Return to *GenAI Studio* and open the GenAI Playground you created earlier.
Make sure RAG is disabled, then ask:

[source,text]
----
What is Fed Aura Capital's maximum back-end DTI ratio for a conventional loan,
and who can approve an exception?
----

The model does not have the internal manual, so a responsible response should say that it cannot verify Fed Aura Capital's policy.
It may instead provide a generic industry threshold or invent a company-specific answer.
Record what happens so you can compare it with the grounded response.

== Exercise 3: Enable RAG and Upload the Policy

In the Playground:

. Open *Configure* and select the *Knowledge* tab.
. Enable RAG.
. Download xref:attachment$fed-aura-internal-policy.txt[the Fed Aura internal policy document].
. Upload `fed-aura-internal-policy.txt` to the Playground knowledge source.
. Wait until the document has finished processing.

== Exercise 4: Ask the Same Question Again

With RAG enabled, send the exact question from Exercise 2 again:

[source,text]
----
What is Fed Aura Capital's maximum back-end DTI ratio for a conventional loan,
and who can approve an exception?
----

The response should now state the 40% company limit and distinguish the approval levels described in the uploaded document.
Inspect the citation or retrieved passage and verify the answer against the source.

For one more retrieval test, ask:

[source,text]
----
How long must Fed Aura retain loan files, HMDA data, and audit trail records?
----

Confirm that the response returns *7 years*, *3 years*, and *10 years*, respectively, and attributes those facts to the uploaded policy.

== Module Summary

=== What You Accomplished

* Experienced document-grounded answers in the Fed Aura application as the CEO
* Asked an internal-policy question without RAG and recorded the unsupported response
* Enabled RAG and uploaded Fed Aura Capital's internal underwriting policy
* Repeated the same question and verified the answer against retrieved source text

=== Key Takeaways

* A model with a good system prompt still cannot answer organization-specific questions without access to private documents
* RAG in the GenAI Playground removes the complexity of setting up vector databases, chunking strategies, and embedding pipelines - you toggle it on and upload files
* RAG-grounded responses reduce regulatory risk by making answers traceable to source documents
* Knowledge updates happen in minutes (upload a new document) instead of days or weeks (retrain the model)
