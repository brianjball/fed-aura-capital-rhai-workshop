Fed Aura Capital
Company Underwriting Manual, Rev. 2024-Q4

DEBT-TO-INCOME OVERLAYS

Fed Aura Capital sets a maximum back-end debt-to-income (DTI) ratio of 40%
for conventional loans. This company overlay is more conservative than the
federal qualified-mortgage safe-harbor threshold of 43%.

Exceptions may be granted by the Chief Credit Officer for borrowers with a
credit score above 760 and verified liquid reserves exceeding 12 months of
housing payments.

UNDERWRITING EXCEPTIONS

Every exception must be documented on the Exception Request Form. A Senior
Underwriter may approve a minor, single-factor exception with low risk. The
Underwriting Manager may approve moderate exceptions, including a DTI override
up to 45% or an LTV override up to two percentage points. The Chief Credit
Officer must approve major exceptions involving multiple factors or a
significant deviation from policy.

Every exception must include a written justification describing the
compensating factors that mitigate the additional risk. Exception approvals
are audited quarterly and reported to the Risk Committee.

RECORD RETENTION

Loan files must be retained for at least seven years from final disposition.
HMDA-related data must be retained for three years after the March 1 submission
deadline. Audit trail records must be retained for ten years.

Electronic document storage must comply with the E-SIGN Act and preserve
document integrity through checksums or digital signatures.
